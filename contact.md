  <h2>What to include in a price correction</h2>
  <p>A price correction is most useful when we can check it. Tell us your city or state, the size of the job, and the material you want priced. For a roof, that means the roof size and the material. For a fence, the length, height, and material. For a move, the home size and the distance. For an oil change, the oil type and the vehicle type. For central air, the home size and the efficiency rating. For a car wrap, the vehicle size and the wrap type. For house painting, the job type and the home size.</p>
  <p>Add the price you were quoted and what was in it, if you have a written quote. Tell us which line looks wrong and why, and link the calculator page if you can. A short message with those facts is far more useful than a long message with no size or place in it, and it lets us check the price against the sources on that page.</p>
  <h2>What happens after you send a message</h2>
  <p>We read every message. We reply to the ones that help us fix or improve the site, such as a price correction with the size and place included, a calculator that gives a wrong result, or a clear idea for a new calculator or guide. Messages that only ask for a price are best answered by the calculators and guides on this site, since a price depends on the size and place of the job.</p>
  <p>If your message helps us fix a price or a calculator, we check it against the sources on that page and update the page. If we need more detail to check it, we will ask for the size, the material, or the place. We do not promise a reply time, and we do not send quotes for your job, since every price on this site is a sample estimated USA range, not a quote.</p>
