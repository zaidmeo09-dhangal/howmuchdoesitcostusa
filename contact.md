        <h2>What to include in a price correction</h2>
        <ul class="checklist">
          <li><strong>Your city or state.</strong></li>
          <li><strong>The size of the job and the material you want priced.</strong> For a roof, the roof size and the material. For a fence, the length, height, and material. For a move, the home size and the distance. For an oil change, the oil type and the vehicle type. For central air, the home size and the efficiency rating. For a car wrap, the vehicle size and the wrap type. For house painting, the job type and the home size.</li>
          <li><strong>The price you were quoted and what was in it.</strong> Tell us which line looks wrong and why, and link the calculator page if you can.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>A short message with those facts is far more useful than a long message with no size or place in it, and it lets us check the price against the sources on that page.</p>

We do not promise a reply time, and we do not send quotes for your job, since every price on this site is a sample estimated USA range, not a quote.</p>
