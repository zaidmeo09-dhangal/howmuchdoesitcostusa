How much do movers cost?

−
A typical 2-bedroom local move costs about $1,240, in a range of $843 to $1,637. The final price depends on home size, distance, and the number of movers and hours.

How do movers calculate cost?

+
Local moves are usually priced by the hour, per mover. Long-distance moves are priced by weight and miles. Home size, stairs, and extra services move the price up or down.

Do movers charge per hour?

+
Yes, local movers usually charge per hour per mover, often with a minimum of 2 to 3 hours. The hourly rate covers the crew, the truck, and basic equipment.

Are the prices quotes or estimates?

+
They are sample estimated USA ranges, not quotes. Use the low, typical, and high to check any quote you receive, and always get 3 quotes from local pros.
