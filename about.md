      <p class="eyebrow">What We Are</p>
      <h2>A focused resource for everyday cost questions</h2>
      <p>Our mission is simple: give every American a straight, honest price before they hire someone. A roof, a fence, a move, or a repaint should not start with a guess or a sales call. It should start with a number you can check, a breakdown you can read, and sources you can open. That is what we build, one calculator and one guide at a time.</p>
      <p>The site covers the jobs Americans price most often before they hire someone. On the home side, that means roof replacement, fences, central air installation, and house painting. On the car side, it means oil changes, car wraps, and car painting. For moving, it means local and long distance moves by home size and distance. Each job has its own calculator and most have a matching guide, so you can get a number fast and then read the detail behind it.</p>
    </div>
    <aside class="visual-card">
      <p class="visual-label">Our approach</p>
      <h3>Useful tools should explain the price, not just display a number.</h3>
      <ul class="visual-list">
        <li><span class="visual-check">&#10003;</span>Simple inputs that match the information you usually have</li>
        <li><span class="visual-check">&#10003;</span>Visible breakdowns and guidance for interpreting the result</li>
        <li><span class="visual-check">&#10003;</span>Layouts that work on phones, tablets and desktop screens</li>
      </ul>

  <div class="section-wrap">
    <div class="section-head">
      <p class="eyebrow">How We Build a Price</p>
      <h2>A practical review process for prices, inputs and results</h2>
      <p class="section-copy">Our mission is simple: give every American a straight, honest price before they hire someone. A roof, a fence, a move, or a repaint should not start with a guess or a sales call. It should start with a number you can check, a breakdown you can read, and sources you can open.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="process-grid">
      <div class="process-card">
        <h3>Start with a typical USA job</h3>
        <p>We start with a typical USA job for the work you want priced.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="process-card">
        <h3>Break it into parts</h3>
        <p>We break it into material, labor, supplies, and equipment.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="process-card">
        <h3>Show low, typical, and high bands</h3>
        <p>We show a low, typical, and high band for each part. The totals you see are the sum of those parts, so you can check our math line by line.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="process-card">
        <h3>Check against sources</h3>
        <p>Our prices are sample estimated USA ranges built from typical material and labor costs, checked against named public sources such as the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, HUD, Harvard JCHS, Home Depot, and Lowes.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <p class="methodology-note">Every calculator page names its own sources in the references section, so you can see where the figures came from. We do not copy prices from other websites and present them as our own. See <a href="{{ '/tools/' | relative_url }}">all 8 cost calculators</a> to check a price.</p>
  </div>
</section>
