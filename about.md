  <h2>Our mission</h2>
  <p>Our mission is simple: give every American a straight, honest price before they hire someone. A roof, a fence, a move, or a repaint should not start with a guess or a sales call. It should start with a number you can check, a breakdown you can read, and sources you can open. That is what we build, one calculator and one guide at a time.</p>
  <h2>How we build a price</h2>
  <ol class="steps">
    <li><strong>Start with a typical USA job.</strong> We start with a typical USA job for the work you want priced.</li>
    <li><strong>Break it into parts.</strong> We break it into material, labor, supplies, and equipment.</li>
    <li><strong>Show low, typical, and high bands.</strong> We show a low, typical, and high band for each part. The totals you see are the sum of those parts, so you can check our math line by line.</li>
  </ol>
  <h2>What we do not do</h2>
  <ul class="checklist checklist-x">
    <li><strong>We do not give quotes.</strong> Every price is a sample estimated USA range, not a quote. Your real price depends on your size, your materials, and where you live, so always get 3 quotes from local pros before you hire anyone.</li>
    <li><strong>We do not sell your data.</strong> All calculations happen in your browser, and nothing you type into a calculator is sent to our servers or stored.</li>
  </ul>
  <h2>Our sources</h2>
  <p>Our prices are sample estimated USA ranges built from typical material and labor costs, checked against named public sources such as the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, HUD, Harvard JCHS, Home Depot, and Lowes. Every calculator page names its own sources in the references section, so you can see where the figures came from. We do not copy prices from other websites and present them as our own.</p>
  <h2>What the site covers</h2>
  <p>The site covers the jobs Americans price most often before they hire someone. On the home side, that means roof replacement, fences, central air installation, and house painting. On the car side, it means oil changes, car wraps, and car painting. For moving, it means local and long distance moves by home size and distance. Each job has its own calculator and most have a matching guide, so you can get a number fast and then read the detail behind it.</p>
  <p>Every guide opens with a direct answer, shows a price table, and links to the matching calculator. The guides cover what drives the price up or down, what is included in the job, and how to compare quotes from local pros. Prices are USA only, and every range is a sample estimated USA range, not a quote.</p>
